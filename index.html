How It Works

One Click to Another World

The WebXR Device API lets us deliver full VR and AR experiences right in your browser. No app store. No downloads. Just presence.

01

Open on any device

Works on Meta Quest browser, Apple Vision, Chrome for Android, and desktop with a WebXR-capable device.

02

Click "Enter VR / AR"

Your browser requests XR session access. Grant permission and your headset or phone enters immersive mode instantly.

03

Interact naturally

Use hand tracking, controllers, or gaze-based input. Your actions shape the experience in real-time.
